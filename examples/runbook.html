Incident response

A P1 incident must be acknowledged within 10 minutes. The incident commander publishes a status update every 30 minutes.

Payment retry policy

Retry transient payment provider failures at most 3 times using exponential backoff with jitter. Do not retry invalid payment credentials. Use an idempotency key to prevent duplicate charges.

Recovery

The database recovery point objective is 15 minutes. The database recovery time objective is 60 minutes. Restore drills run quarterly.
