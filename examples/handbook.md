# Acme employee handbook

## Annual leave
Acme employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year. The leave year begins on January 1. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused leave days into the next calendar year. Carried leave must be used by March 31.

## Remote work
Acme employees may work remotely for 3 days per week with manager approval. Work from another country requires written approval from HR before travel.

## Equipment
The home office equipment allowance is 500 credits per employee per year. Expense claims require a receipt and manager approval.
